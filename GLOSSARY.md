# intuneme

Language for the container identity and enrollment choices managed by intuneme.

## Identity

**Identity stack**:
The account, authentication broker, and compliance services used by a container to sign users in and establish device identity.

**Image family**:
A mutually exclusive container rootfs choice bound to one identity stack and its enrollment state.

**Himmelblau mode**:
The experimental identity stack that uses Himmelblau in place of Microsoft's broker and Intune Portal.
